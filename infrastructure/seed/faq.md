# Glanz AG FAQ

## Do you clean offices outside normal hours?

Yes. Office cleaning can be scheduled early morning, evening, or weekend,
depending on crew availability.

## Can you provide a price during the call?

Yes. Atrium can provide a ballpark estimate once service type, area, room count,
urgency, and timing are known.

## Do you need photos?

For complex construction cleaning or unusual window jobs, photos may be requested
after the call before a final quote is confirmed.
